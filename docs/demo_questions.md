# Demo Questions — Agile HR Copilot

These are safe, repeatable questions for the local demo and interview walkthrough.

## 1. Executive narrative
Generate a CHRO-ready narrative for Q3 2026 using the current KPI values.

## 2. Retention policy
What does our retention policy say about employees with elevated flight risk?

## 3. Career growth
What should a manager do if a long-tenure employee has not been promoted recently?

## 4. Risk explanation
Explain the attrition risk for one high-risk employee in plain English.

## 5. Governance
How does this project protect employee privacy in dashboards and AI answers?
